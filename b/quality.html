The science

Three active ingredients, three jobs.

WasabiRub layers a cooling active, a warming active and a topical analgesic. Together they create the cool-then-warm sensation the formula is known for.

The cool signal

Menthol · 10%

Activates the skin’s cold-sensing receptors (TRPM8), producing an immediate cooling sensation that helps take the edge off how soreness feels.

The warm signal

Capsaicin · .0375%

The compound behind chili-pepper heat works on heat-sensitive nerve endings (TRPV1) to build a gradual, lasting warming sensation where you apply it.

The relief

Methyl salicylate · 20%

A salicylate related to aspirin, it works topically as a counterirritant to temporarily relieve minor aches and pains of muscles and joints.

Uses: for the temporary relief of minor aches and pains of muscles and joints. Sensation and intensity vary by person and application area. Always read and follow the Drug Facts label.

The professional protocol

How the pros put it to work.

The same routine athletes and sports-medicine staff follow — one application to prep, one to recover.

Before · Prep for game time

Prime the muscle

Apply sparingly 10–15 minutes before activity to activate muscle tissue, increase blood flow, and reduce stiffness. WasabiRub goes on cool, then heats up — massage deeply to stimulate the heating effect.

After · Post-game recovery

Recover faster

Use immediately after activity to help reduce soreness and inflammation and support faster recovery — so you’re ready for the next session.

Pro tip for activation

Massage deeply into the tissue using gloved hands, a massage tool, or after applying a warm towel to the area. This increases local blood flow and enhances the heating effect for better performance and mobility.

For external use only. Use only as directed. Sensation and intensity vary by person and application area.
