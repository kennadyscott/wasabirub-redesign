How we work

What “pharmacy-grade”
actually means here.

Anyone can print the phrase on a label. These are the specific checks a product passes before it reaches an athlete — and the documentation that follows it.

Compounded medications require a patient-specific prescription and are not FDA-approved products. Sport status can depend on the ingredient, route, dose and governing body.

Standards

Six checks, every time.

Applied to compounding, repackaging and anything we dispense.

  • USP-compliant compoundingPrepared using USP <795>/<797> techniques and ingredients, in a licensed facility.
  • Batch documentationBeyond-use date, storage conditions and lot numbers on every label, with records retained.
  • Pharmacist verificationA final check for accuracy, quality, documentation and labeling before anything ships.
  • Pharmaceutical-grade ingredientsSourced for compounding, not repackaged from consumer supply.
  • Anti-doping reviewFormulations checked against WADA, USADA and NCAA requirements, with TUE support where needed.
  • TruShield™ certificationThird-party certification on the topical range, with documentation your compliance staff can file.

Athletes are ultimately responsible for what they ingest. We support informed, risk-aware decision-making.

Code of ethics and values

Four principles,
in that order.

In pursuing our mission to advance pharmaceutical excellence in sports medicine, SportPharm™ conducts its business guided by the following principles.

About SportPharm →
  1. 01
    Operate with integrity (obey the law)Compliance is the foundation to all business activity. We must adhere to all pharmacy and medical laws, regulations, and ethical standards, ensuring transparency and accountability in every action.
  2. 02
    Empower our teamWe foster a culture of respect, growth, and collaboration, recognizing that our people are our greatest asset.
  3. 03
    Prioritize our partners and patientsWe are dedicated to delivering safe, effective, and evidence-based solutions that protect and enhance the health and safety of every patient we serve.
  4. 04
    Respect our collaborators and suppliersWe build lasting, honest relationships with those who share our commitment to quality, innovation, and ethical business practices.
