MADE SIMPLE.

Three active ingredients.
One smart formula.

01

THE COOL SIGNAL

MENTHOL 10%

Menthol activates TRPM8—cold-sensitive receptors in the skin—creating an immediate cooling sensation that can temporarily shift attention away from soreness.

02

THE HEAT SIGNAL

CAPSAICIN .0375%

Capsaicin activates heat-sensitive TRPV1 receptors. The first warm or tingling feeling gives way to local desensitization with continued use, quieting the intensity of pain signaling.

03

THE TARGETED SIGNAL

METHYL SALICYLATE 20%

Methyl salicylate is a topical counterirritant used for temporary relief of minor muscle and joint aches—delivering localized action right where it is applied.

FROM PROFESSIONAL
CLUBHOUSES TO YOURS.

WasabiRub was developed by Robert Nickell, the first sports pharmacist for the U.S. Olympic Team. It first found a following with professional league pitchers in the late ’90s, then spread through college, minor league, and high school locker rooms.

“It goes on cool. As your body warms up, so does the rub.”

Today, that same pharmacist-formulated blend is made for athletic trainers, sports medicine professionals, and active individuals who take recovery seriously.
