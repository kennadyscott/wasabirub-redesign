Product schema on the three product pagesBlocking

Every page now carries a canonical, Open Graph tags and a built 1200×630 share card, plus valid FAQPage markup that matches the visible copy exactly. What is missing is Product and Offer markup — price, availability, brand.

Why it matters more than the FAQ: Google restricted FAQ rich results to government and health sites in 2023, so the FAQ markup is unlikely to produce rich results for a consumer brand however well it is built. Product and Offer markup still does. For a shop page it is the single highest-value piece of structured data, and it is the one we do not have.

What unblocks itTwo answers already on this list. Confirmed prices, because the markup states a figure; and the store question, because availability is meaningless while Add to Cart goes nowhere. Answer those two and this is an hour of work.
