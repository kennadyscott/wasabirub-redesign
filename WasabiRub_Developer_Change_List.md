# WasabiRub Website Redesign — Developer Change List

**Reference pages**

- Original homepage: `https://kennadyscott.github.io/sportpharm-site/wasabirub.html`
- Original product page: `https://kennadyscott.github.io/sportpharm-site/wasabirub-product.html`
- Primary design reference: AG1 (`https://drinkag1.com/`)

## 1. Overall direction

- Make the site cleaner, simpler, more premium, and more trustworthy.
- Reduce visual clutter, excessive headings, badges, decorative lines, competing calls to action, and “salesy” elements that can make the product feel fake or scam-like.
- Use AG1 as the primary inspiration for spacing, hierarchy, product presentation, ingredient storytelling, and restrained visual design. Do not copy AG1 literally.
- Position WasabiRub as a **premium clinical-performance topical relief brand**: professional sports-medicine expertise made relevant and relatable to everyday active people.
- Balance authority with humanity. The brand should be credible to pharmacists, clinicians, athletic trainers, sports-medicine professionals, and retail buyers while still feeling friendly and relatable to consumers.
- Audience range should feel inclusive: from a gym dad or group-fitness regular to a marathoner, former college athlete, Ironman trainee, or professional cyclist.

## 2. Brand architecture and terminology

- Make **WasabiRub** the main consumer-facing brand and SportPharm's hero product/masterbrand.
- SportPharm should move into an endorsement role, such as **“Created by SportPharm pharmacists”**, instead of dominating the website identity.
- Bring the whole topical line under WasabiRub:
  - WasabiRub Original
  - WasabiRub Super Hot
  - WasabiRub Super Cold
  - WasabiRub Lidocaine (final product name and regulatory language to be confirmed)
- Replace older consumer-facing references to “IcetraRub” with “WasabiRub Super Cold” where the rebrand is approved.
- Core message direction:
  - **Trusted in training rooms. Made for every body that moves.**
  - **Professional-grade relief for real life.**
  - **Serious expertise. Real people in your corner.**

## 3. Global navigation

- Remove the SportPharm logo from the corner/header because the destination will ultimately be `wasabirub.com`.
- Move the main navigation to the left.
- Keep the navigation clean and consistent across the WasabiRub homepage and product pages.
- Suggested top-level items:
  - Shop
  - Why WasabiRub
  - Quality
  - Athlete Hub
  - For Professionals
  - Cart
- Add product images to the Shop dropdown.
- Use transparent product cutouts in the dropdown rather than square white-background thumbnails.
- Product dropdown naming should follow the WasabiRub masterbrand system.

## 4. Homepage hero

- Remove the red decorative line next to the original “Triple Action Sports Relief” header.
- Replace the original header image with a real, premium product image.
- Current preferred visual direction: use the real open-jar WasabiRub close-up as an editorial hero image that blends into the dark navy background.
- Avoid placing the hero image inside a stark white box.
- The image should feel integrated with the background using a restrained crop, soft gradient treatment, rounded editorial panel, or similar premium solution.
- Hero copy should reflect the revised brand positioning:
  - Eyebrow: **Professional-grade relief for real life**
  - Main headline may remain **Feel It Work.**
  - Supporting idea: pharmacist-created relief trusted in training rooms and made for every body that moves.
- Use one clear primary button: **Shop WasabiRub**.
- Keep the hero clean. Avoid floating badges, orbit graphics, price bubbles, or multiple competing actions.

## 5. Trust signals below the hero

- Remove the original small trust badges from the hero itself:
  - TruShield Certified
  - Pharmacist Formulated
  - Five-star review proof
- Place the strongest trust signals in a clean band directly below the hero with larger, more readable text.
- Recommended three proof points:
  - TruShield Certified / quality tested
  - Pharmacist created or pharmacist formulated
  - Trusted by athletes and sports-medicine professionals
- Ensure these claims are accurate, current, and legally approved.

## 6. Product selector: “How do you want relief to feel?”

- Move this section higher on the homepage.
- Remove:
  - “Your recovery. Your sensation.”
  - “From pure cooling to serious heat, there's a SportPharm rub built for your recovery style.”
- Use a white section background.
- Show three clear product cards so users do not have to infer that tabs or sensation labels are clickable.
- Use distinct but restrained product-family colors:
  - Green: WasabiRub Original
  - Red: WasabiRub Super Hot
  - Blue: WasabiRub Super Cold
- Each card should contain:
  - Product name
  - Short description
  - Sensation/intensity cue
  - Price, if approved/current
  - Clear Shop Now action
  - Real product image
- Product images must be transparent PNG cutouts so the jars sit naturally on the colored cards without white rectangular backgrounds.

## 7. Ingredient/formula section

- Replace the older “Most rubs pick one lane. WasabiRub chose three.” treatment with a cleaner, more AG1-like ingredient presentation.
- Keep the overall message, but remove unnecessary decorative lines and visual clutter.
- Use a dark navy section background, consistent with the hero/header color.
- Present three ingredient cards:
  - Capsaicin — chili pepper
  - Menthol — mint/menthol leaf
  - Methyl salicylate — oil of wintergreen
- Use simple, editorial ingredient photography:
  - One chili pepper, not a group of peppers
  - One clean mint sprig or leaf
  - One clean wintergreen sprig/berry composition
  - White or very light neutral photographic backdrops
  - No green-tinted or busy image backgrounds
- Keep descriptions short and easy to understand.
- Recommended information:
  - Ingredient name
  - Percentage, based on the current product label
  - Sensation/function cue: warm, cool, or target
  - One-sentence explanation
- Avoid unsupported mechanistic or medical claims.

## 8. Testimonials

- Align testimonial content to the left.
- Show three testimonials at a time on desktop.
- Use clean cards with consistent height and restrained styling.
- Prioritize a mix of professional credibility and relatable voices where verified testimonials are available.
- Professional proof can include athletic trainers, sports-medicine clinicians, pharmacists, and professionals working with athletes.
- The tone should communicate: clinicians trust the authority, and consumers like and relate to the people behind the brand.

## 9. Professional/athlete story section

- Remove the red line next to “Why It Feels Different.”
- Simplify the original “Most rubs pick one lane…” athlete section.
- If a lifestyle image is used, place text on the left and the athlete image on the right.
- Use a real, credible athlete or practitioner image rather than a highly staged stock image.
- Use a simplified ingredient/benefit list and one Shop Now button.
- The lifestyle spectrum should feel broad and relatable: everyday active adults through professional athletes.

## 10. Quality and certification

- Add a dedicated quality section with a strong, concise headline.
- Include the TruShield logo and explain the certification in simple language.
- Emphasize that the company is created and operated by pharmacists.
- Present quality testing and professional trust as reasons for the premium price.
- Keep the design clinical and premium, not crowded with seals or badges.
- Only use “clean,” “independently tested,” “banned-substance screened,” “Made in USA,” certification, or similar language when substantiated and approved.

## 11. Featured products banner

- Replace the shorter “OG Heat Duo” block with a longer, full-width **Featured Products** banner.
- Keep the layout simple and premium.
- Use transparent product cutouts rather than product images trapped inside a white box.
- A restrained tonal or radial background is preferred over plain white.
- Include one clear product story and one primary Shop action.

## 12. Proud partners

- Add a Proud Partners section for:
  - USA Handball
  - Nexus Sports Medicine Bags
  - UFC
- Use official partner logos only when permission and correct assets are available.
- Do not imply league/team sponsorship beyond the actual approved relationship.

## 13. Homepage content and layout cleanup

- Reduce the overall number of headings and repeated eyebrow labels.
- Remove decorative red rules that do not add meaning.
- Use generous whitespace and one primary message per section.
- Avoid repeating the same proof points in multiple places.
- Keep sections shorter so the page feels focused rather than endless.
- Use dark navy, white/warm neutral, controlled green, red, and cooling blue.
- Red should be an action/accent color, not a dominant page background everywhere.

## 14. Product page redesign

- Redesign `wasabirub-product.html` to feel elevated, legitimate, clinical, and premium.
- Match the homepage navigation and WasabiRub masterbrand naming.
- Reduce excessive headers, badges, icons, offer boxes, competing sections, and sales pressure.
- Above-the-fold layout:
  - Left half: one simple, large product image with generous white margin
  - Right half: product title, concise description, proof/review line if verified, price/options, and a clear Add to Cart button
- Use only one main WasabiRub product image in the primary gallery view.
- Do not use multiple busy ingredient images in the primary product area.
- If ingredient imagery appears later, use the same clean, simple chili pepper, mint, and wintergreen images chosen for the homepage.
- Product page section order:
  1. Title and concise description
  2. Large simple product image + purchase panel
  3. Who it's for
  4. Benefits
  5. How to use
  6. Quality and certification
  7. Comparison chart
  8. Testimonials
  9. FAQ
- Use white space and clear hierarchy so the product looks trustworthy rather than overly promotional.
- Keep one obvious Add to Cart action.

## 15. Product comparison

- Add a clear comparison chart for the WasabiRub family.
- Compare only useful decision factors, such as:
  - Product/variant
  - Sensation
  - Active ingredients and percentages from current labels
  - Best-fit use case
  - Relative intensity
- Avoid unsupported superiority claims.

## 16. Voice and relatability

- The brand voice should be:
  - Clinical, never cold
  - Premium, never precious
  - Expert, never complicated
  - Warm, relatable, and never overly corporate
- Content should feature real people and useful education from pharmacists, clinicians, athletic trainers, physical therapists, and everyday active consumers.
- The people behind the brand should feel visible and likable, not anonymous authorities.
- Communicate professional knowledge in plain language.
- Avoid macho sports language, miracle claims, fear tactics, or aggressive “destroy pain” messaging.

## 17. Credibility and claims guardrails

- Verify all product names, formulas, active percentages, prices, certifications, and Drug Facts against current approved materials before launch.
- Verify and obtain permission before using:
  - USA team or league names
  - MLB or NHL references
  - UFC name/logo
  - University names/logos
  - Professional athlete or clinician endorsements
  - Partner logos
- Do not imply official sponsorship or broad league endorsement when the relationship is with an individual team, practitioner, or customer.
- Avoid saying the product heals injuries, speeds healing, prevents injury, or provides results beyond the approved OTC label.
- Keep directions, warnings, and Drug Facts easy to find.

## 18. Responsive and accessibility requirements

- Preserve a clean hierarchy on tablet and mobile.
- Stack the hero copy above the image on smaller screens.
- Ensure transparent product cutouts remain large and readable without being cropped.
- Product cards and testimonial cards should stack cleanly on mobile.
- Shop dropdown must be keyboard accessible and usable on touch devices.
- Use meaningful alt text for informative images; decorative images may use empty alt text.
- Maintain WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic headings, and large enough tap targets.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid unnecessary animation.
