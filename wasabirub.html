OUR QUALITY STANDARD

Good to know

QUESTIONS,
ANSWERED.

Straightforward guidance before you choose your rub. Always read and follow the Drug Facts label.

What does WasabiRub feel like?

WasabiRub begins with a cooling sensation from menthol, followed by gradual warmth from capsaicin. Sensation and intensity can vary by person and application area.

When should I apply it?

Apply to the affected area as directed on the Drug Facts label. Many customers use it around training or whenever minor muscle and joint aches slow them down.

Is it tested for athletes?

WasabiRub carries TruShield certification. Visit our quality page for details about the testing standard and certification.

Which product should I choose?

Choose WasabiRub for balanced cool and heat, WasabiRub Super Hot for more intense warmth, or WasabiRub Super Cold for focused cooling without heat.

Can I use it with a heating pad or wrap?

No. Do not use WasabiRub with a heating pad, bandage or wrap, and avoid external heat after applying. This is on the Drug Facts label for a reason.

Do I need a prescription?

No. WasabiRub is an over-the-counter product.
