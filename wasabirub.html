FAQ

How often can I apply WasabiRub?

Follow the directions on the Drug Facts label. Do not apply more often than directed, and stop use if irritation develops.

Will WasabiRub stain clothing or leave a scent?

It is a gel that rubs in. As with any topical containing menthol and methyl salicylate, there is a noticeable scent on application that fades.

Can I use WasabiRub with a heating pad?

No. Do not use with a heating pad, bandage or wrap, and avoid external heat after applying. This is on the label for a reason.

What is the difference between the three rubs?

WasabiRub is the balanced triple-action formula. Super Hot doubles the capsaicin for more heat. Super Cold is menthol-led for pure cooling. The comparison above sets them side by side.

Is WasabiRub available without a prescription?

Yes. WasabiRub is sold over the counter, with no prescription needed.

Who should I ask if I am not sure WasabiRub suits me?

Ask a pharmacist, particularly if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking other medicines, or applying it to a large area. Read the full Drug Facts label before use.
